## Core Pain Points Solved

### Non-US founder Stripe bans: why buying fake accounts can destroy your runway

A purchased “verified” account, borrowed identity or invented US business address is a fragile foundation for recurring revenue. Stripe requires accurate business information and can suspend access or require reserves. Incorporation does not bypass verification, country restrictions or underwriting. [Stripe service terms](https://stripe.com/legal/ssa-services-terms), [Stripe agreement and reserve definition](https://stripe.com/legal/ssa).

**The 120-day fund-hold problem:** imagine $10,000 of your runway locked away for 120 days while your hosting, payroll and refunds still need cash. That is a cash-flow stress scenario, not a claim that every non-US founder, fake account or closure receives a standard 120-day hold. Release timing is account-specific; no formation provider can promise Stripe approval or release of funds.

Build a legitimate route: an eligible MoR seller account, or a real entity with truthful ownership information and suitable banking. Compare [Lemon Squeezy](https://go.272738.xyz/lemonsqueezy), [Paddle](https://go.272738.xyz/paddle) and [Doola](https://go.272738.xyz/doola) before putting your runway behind a checkout.

### Global Sales Tax / VAT compliance hell in 2026

Selling globally can expose you to **40+ US state sales-tax regimes** and EU customer-location VAT rules. It does **not** mean every founder must immediately register in every state: nexus thresholds, product taxability and customer location matter. Once obligations arise, collection alone is not the same as registration, filing and remittance. [US sales tax and economic nexus guide](https://stripe.com/guides/introduction-to-us-sales-tax-and-economic-nexus).

For EU consumer services, the non-Union OSS can simplify VAT reporting for qualifying suppliers established outside the EU. It does not make the VAT disappear. [EU One Stop Shop guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/finance-and-tax/vat/one-stop-shop/indexamp_en.htm).

A MoR takes responsibility for indirect tax on the sales for which it is the merchant. You still have your own business-registration and income-tax duties. With native Stripe, **your business is the merchant**; an LLC is not a sales-tax solution. [Lemon Squeezy tax responsibilities](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/payments/sales-tax-vat), [Paddle's MoR offering](https://www.paddle.com/pricing).

Missing a required IRS Form 5472 can carry a **$25,000 penalty**. Foreign-owned US disregarded entities with applicable reportable transactions generally attach Form 5472 to a pro forma Form 1120. Filing requirements depend on your facts; a zero-income business is not automatically exempt. [IRS Form 5472 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472).

**The practical choice:** pay a MoR premium to simplify covered sales-tax obligations, or budget for the actual work behind your own merchant account. [Run your numbers first](https://pay.mybskm.top).

## The Real Math: MoR vs Stripe + US LLC

### Transparent base model

Let `R` be monthly revenue (MRR), `A` average order value (AOV), and `B` your annual LLC compliance budget.

```text
Monthly transactions N       = R / A
MoR monthly cost              = R × 5%   + N × $0.50
Stripe processing             = R × 2.9% + N × $0.30
LLC monthly compliance        = B / 12
Self-managed monthly total    = Stripe processing + B / 12
Net monthly difference Δ      = MoR monthly cost − self-managed monthly total
Annualized cost               = unrounded monthly cost × 12
```

**If Δ ≤ 0:** highlight Lemon Squeezy and Paddle as **Early Stage Winner** (equal base-rate estimates). **If Δ > 0:** highlight **US LLC + Stripe (Doola)** as **Scale Winner**, with modeled annual savings of `12 × Δ`.

Default `B = $1,000/year`, or **$83.33/month displayed**. Calculations retain the full `1000 / 12` precision. The budget allows for applicable **Franchise Tax / annual state reports, Registered Agent, and IRS 5472 / pro forma 1120 preparation**. State charges and filing obligations vary. It is a planning input, not a universal legal cost or a Doola quote.

**Important price distinction:** Doola currently lists Starter at **$297/year plus state fees**, and Tax and Compliance at **$1,999/year plus state fees**. The default $1,000 budget does not purchase that tax package. Enter your actual combined recurring quote, without double-counting services. [Doola published plans](https://www.doola.com/pricing/).

### What the base rates include—and leave out

- **Lemon Squeezy:** published 5% + $0.50 base fee. Its published surcharges include **+1.5% international**, **+1.5% PayPal**, and **+0.5% subscriptions**. Payout charges can apply; the fee can be based on the checkout amount including tax. [Official fee breakdown](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees).
- **Paddle:** published pay-as-you-go checkout rate **5% + $0.50**, including its MoR offering; negotiated plans may differ. [Published pricing](https://www.paddle.com/pricing).
- **Native Stripe:** **2.9% + $0.30** is the US domestic-card base rate. International cards, conversion, Billing, Tax and other optional products can add costs. This comparison concerns native Payments, not Stripe's separate MoR offering. [US pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing).

The arithmetic treats MRR as the modeled processing base and assumes stable revenue for 12 months. It excludes collected sales tax/VAT, income taxes, one-time formation, FX, refunds, disputes, payout fees, separate billing products and sales-tax registration/software/filing costs. **This is a base-rate decision model, not an all-in global checkout quote.** Add recurring self-managed costs to the annual budget; model one-time costs separately. Expected transaction counts may be fractional; we do not round them before computing fees.

### The true break-even calculation

```text
Δ = R × (0.021 + 0.20 / A) − B / 12
Break-even monthly revenue R* = (B / 12) / (0.021 + 0.20 / A)
```

With the default $1,000/year allowance:

| AOV | Break-even MRR (rounded) |
| ---: | ---: |
| $10 | $2,032.52 |
| $40 | $3,205.13 |
| $100 | $3,623.19 |

At exact equality, the calculator selects MoR. Above the unrounded threshold, native Stripe + LLC wins on this modeled cost alone. If `B = $0`, the threshold is $0 and Stripe wins for positive modeled revenue. If real compliance costs rise, the threshold rises proportionally.

At $40 AOV, a **$1,999/year** budget moves break-even to **$6,407.05 MRR**, before any additional state fees or other excluded costs. Changing the budget is a more useful decision than declaring “switch at $7,500” for everyone.
