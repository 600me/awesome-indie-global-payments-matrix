The model compares a Merchant of Record at 5% + $0.50 per order with Stripe at 2.9% + $0.30, adding 1.5% for international cards, 1% for non-USD conversion and an editable annual US LLC compliance budget divided by twelve. It is a planning estimate, not a quote, a tax opinion or a guarantee of eligibility. Provider pricing, state fees and filing duties change; verify them before committing.
