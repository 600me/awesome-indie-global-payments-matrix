01 / ACCOUNT RISK

A fake Stripe account can cost your runway.

Borrowed identities and “verified” accounts can fail verification and trigger suspension or reserves. Imagine $10,000 inaccessible for 120 days: that is four months of cash-flow pain. This is a risk scenario, not a universal Stripe hold policy.

02 / TAX EXPOSURE

Global checkout means local obligations.

40+ US state tax regimes, customer-location VAT rules and filing deadlines can turn a low headline fee into a compliance bill. A MoR handles indirect tax on its sales; an LLC alone does not. Your obligations depend on nexus, product and customer location.

Open the math, assumptions & verification notes
  • N = MRR / AOV. Fractional orders are expected averages; transaction counts are not rounded before calculation.
  • MoR = MRR × 0.05 + N × $0.50 (each provider separately). Stripe = MRR × 0.029 + N × $0.30 + annual budget / 12.
  • Net difference = MoR − Stripe. A nonpositive result selects both MoRs; a positive result selects US LLC + Stripe. Break-even MRR = (annual budget / 12) / (0.021 + 0.20 / AOV). Annual figures use unrounded monthly costs × 12.
  • USD baseline assuming stable monthly revenue. No international-card, currency conversion, subscriptions/Billing add-ons, refunds, disputes, payout fees, formation fees, collected sales tax/VAT or income taxes are included. This is not an all-in quote or a profit forecast.
  • Lemon Squeezy lists +1.5% international, +1.5% PayPal and +0.5% subscription surcharges. Its fee can apply to tax-inclusive checkout totals. Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 is a US domestic-card rate. These are materially different products.
  • Base pricing checked against provider pages in 2026 (2026-10-16). Doola currently lists Tax and Compliance at $1,999/year plus state fees; the editable $1,000 budget is a planning assumption, not that package. Source links and the full comparison are in the decision matrix.
  • Shared links preserve MRR, AOV and a nondefault annual budget. Invalid values are clamped to supported bounds; range values snap to their steps. No cookies, analytics, external libraries or network calls are needed for the calculator.
