01 / ACCOUNT RISK

A fake Stripe account can cost your runway.

Borrowed identities and “verified” accounts can fail verification and trigger suspension or reserves. Imagine $10,000 inaccessible for 120 days: that is four months of cash-flow pain. This is a risk scenario, not a universal Stripe hold policy.

02 / TAX EXPOSURE

Global checkout means local obligations.

40+ US state tax regimes, customer-location VAT rules and filing deadlines can turn a low headline fee into a compliance bill. A MoR handles indirect tax on its sales; an LLC alone does not. Your obligations depend on nexus, product and customer location.

Open the math, assumptions & verification notes
  • N = MRR / AOV. Fractional orders are expected averages; transaction counts are not rounded before calculation.
  • MoR = MRR × 0.05 + N × $0.50 (each provider separately). StripeRate = 2.9% + (Intl / 100) × 1.5% + (FX ? 1% : 0). Stripe = MRR × StripeRate + N × $0.30 + annual budget / 12.
  • Net difference = MoR − Stripe. A nonpositive result selects both MoRs; a positive result selects US LLC + Stripe. RateDelta = (5% − StripeRate) + (0.20 / AOV); Break-even MRR = RateDelta > 0 ? (annual budget / 12) / RateDelta : Infinity. Annual figures use unrounded monthly costs × 12.
  • Defaults are intl=0 and fx=0, so a URL without those parameters keeps the original US domestic-card baseline. Each preset button reloads $8,000 MRR, $40 AOV, $1,000/year with its card mix: 0% Intl → $3,205/mo break-even; 70% Intl → $5,376/mo; 70% Intl + 1% FX → $15,152/mo. URL parameters intl (0–100, step 5) and fx (0/1) round-trip through the share link.
  • USD reporting baseline assuming stable monthly revenue. The model still excludes subscriptions/Billing add-ons, refunds, disputes, payout fees, formation fees, collected sales tax/VAT and income taxes. International card and FX surcharges are modeled only through the two controls above; real processor pricing varies by card country, currency and account agreement.
  • Lemon Squeezy lists +1.5% international, +1.5% PayPal and +0.5% subscription surcharges. Its fee can apply to tax-inclusive checkout totals. Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 is a US domestic-card rate. These are materially different products.
  • Base pricing checked against provider pages in 2026 (2026-10-16). Firstbase lists a $399 company-formation package before state fees and add-ons; the editable $1,000/year budget is a separate compliance planning allowance, not a Firstbase quote. Doola currently lists Tax and Compliance at $1,999/year plus state fees. Source links and the full comparison are in the decision matrix and the breakeven research note.
